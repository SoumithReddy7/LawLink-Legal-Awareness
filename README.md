# ⚖️ LawLink – Gamified Legal Literacy Platform

> Making legal awareness simple, interactive, and accessible.

LawLink is a web-based **Gamified Legal Literacy Platform** designed to help users explore basic legal concepts through simplified learning content, real-life scenarios, interactive quizzes, and progress-based gamification.

The project was developed as part of **Hackober – Google Developer Groups on Campus, GRIET** by **Team Evolve**.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Legal information can often feel complex or difficult to approach. LawLink aims to make the learning experience more engaging by combining legal awareness with interactive learning and gamification.

Users can explore legal topics, understand practical scenarios, test their knowledge through quizzes, and track their learning progress through the platform.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔐 **User Authentication**
  - Registration
  - Login
  - Password recovery
  - Protected application routes

- 📚 **Legal Learning**
  - Explore legal topics
  - Topic-specific learning content
  - Real-life legal scenarios

- 🧠 **Interactive Quizzes**
  - Multiple-choice questions
  - Quiz results and feedback
  - Progress tracking

- 🎮 **Gamification**
  - XP-based progression
  - Levels
  - Badges
  - Progress tracking
  - Leaderboard

- 📊 **Personal Dashboard**
  - Learning progress
  - User information
  - Gamification progress

- 📖 **Resources**
  - Practical legal resources and information

- 🤖 **Assistant**
  - Dedicated assistant interface for legal-awareness support

- 📱 **Responsive UI**
  - Modern interface designed for different screen sizes
